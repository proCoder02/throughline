04 Where we are now

Standing — 2026-10-03
  • Phase 0 is fully locked — all three task pairs defined against real code, rate card fixed, accuracy floors set.
  • Phase 2's case study is effectively done: CostLens's feature-tag allowlist shows Aggregation would be correctly attributed, Classification and Retrieval would not — a real, code-verified opacity finding, not a hypothetical.
  • Retrieval's accuracy grader just got fixed and re-validated on production (100/100), after a first attempt (LLM-as-judge) was tested and found unreliable.
  • Classification is fully measured — all three tiers, five trials each, $0.32 total spend — and surfaced a genuine bug in production's own regex.
  • Immediate next steps: Retrieval's N=100/1,000 on production; Aggregation's first run; pulling Grafana's real "untagged" cost totals as Phase 2 corroboration.
