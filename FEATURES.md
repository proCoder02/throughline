# Throughline — Feature Overview

Throughline is a personal-intelligence app: it listens to your conversations, remembers the people and things that come up in them, and turns that into useful help — tasks, answers, relationship context, and a way to actually talk to your friends. It's available as a **mobile app** (the primary experience) and a **web app** (a lighter companion), backed by one shared account and one shared history.

---

## 1. Listen & Transcribe
The core feature: record a conversation live and get an accurate, speaker-separated transcript as it happens.
- Start a "Listen" session and see the transcript appear line by line, with each speaker automatically told apart.
- If a new voice joins mid-conversation, you're prompted to name them — either picking someone you've talked about before or entering a new name.
- Come back to a conversation later and keep recording into it, instead of starting a new one.
- While listening, the app quietly surfaces topic chips and follow-up questions worth asking, which you can tap to ask immediately.
- *(Mobile)* A pulsing "recording" indicator with a live timer stays visible while a session is running, so you never lose track of an active recording.

## 2. Conversations & AI Q&A
Every recorded (or manually saved) conversation becomes something you can search and interrogate later.
- Browse all your past conversations, organized and filterable by category (Personal / Office / Study / your own custom categories).
- Ask questions about one specific conversation ("what did we agree on?") and get answers grounded in that transcript.
- Ask a separate, global assistant questions that span *everything* you've ever recorded or discussed — "what did I talk about with Rahul last week?"
- Attach a photo to a question and get it analyzed as part of the answer.
- Ask something location-flavored ("find a coffee shop near me") and get real nearby suggestions folded into the answer.
- Full-text search across every conversation's transcript, with matching snippets highlighted.
- Delete conversations you don't want to keep.

## 3. Tasks
Things you agreed to do or were asked to do get pulled out of your conversations automatically, without you writing them down.
- Tasks are auto-extracted from your recordings and chats.
- View, filter (open / done / all), and organize them by category.
- Mark complete, reopen, edit the description or due date, or delete.
- Jump straight back to the conversation a task came from.
- Get a reminder email (with a calendar invite) before a task is due.

## 4. People (Profiles)
The app remembers who you've talked about or talked with, even if they're not a friend on the platform.
- Anyone mentioned or heard in your recordings gets a running profile: what's been noted about them, organized by category, each note linked back to the conversation it came from.
- Search your people, rename someone if a name was guessed wrong, or delete a profile you don't want tracked.

## 5. Friends & Direct Messaging
A real messaging layer for people who also use the app.
- Add friends using a shareable friend code.
- Chat 1:1 in real time — text, photos, videos, and files, with delivered/read receipts and typing indicators.
- Each friend has a profile screen showing your call history, their current mood, and quick actions to message or call them.
- Messages you send while offline are queued and sent automatically once you're back online.
- Unread messages and calls are badged so nothing gets missed.

## 6. Voice Calls
Calling built directly into the app, tied into the same transcript/AI pipeline as everything else.
- Place or join 1:1 and group voice calls with your friends.
- Mute, decline, or leave a call; get notified if you're dropped.
- *(Mobile)* Minimize an active call to a small pill and keep browsing the rest of the app while still on the line.
- *(Mobile)* Calls ring you natively — even if the app isn't open — using the phone's own call-screen experience.
- Calls you actually have a conversation on are automatically transcribed afterward and show up alongside your other conversations, tasks, and profile notes.

## 7. Mood Tracking & Weekly Insights
The app keeps a light pulse on how you've been feeling, based on your conversations.
- A day-by-day mood trend view (a monthly heatmap) with a streak badge for how consistently you've checked in.
- See a friend's recent mood on their profile (only if they've chosen to share it).
- A weekly "digest" of AI-derived insights about your habits, preferences, mood swings, and relationships, presented as a swipeable card stack.

## 8. Cognitive Sharing (relationship intelligence)
An opt-in feature for getting more out of a friendship than a plain chat log.
- Each friend relationship has a sharing level you both control: off, limited, or fully collaborative.
- When both sides opt in, the app can surface "common ground" suggestions — things you two might want to talk about or do together — right inside your chat.
- Shared file/photo attachments can get a short AI-written summary.

## 9. Cognitive Commerce (in-chat ordering)
Ask the assistant for something and let it actually help you get it, without ever ordering on its own.
- Ask about food (or, where enabled, groceries/dining) inside a chat, and the assistant can search real options and show you a pick-an-item card.
- Nothing is ever ordered without your explicit confirmation.
- Once confirmed, pay by UPI or cash and track the order's progress live, right inside the conversation.
- Connect or disconnect your food-delivery account at any time from Settings.

## 10. Notifications
Kept in the loop in real time, in-app and via push.
- Live updates for incoming calls, new tasks, unread messages, mood updates from friends, and finished recordings/digests.
- Push notifications (even when the app is closed) that deep-link straight to the relevant screen.
- *(Mobile)* Task reminders can be actioned directly from the notification.

## 11. Personalization & Settings
- A short one-time onboarding captures who you are (age, interests, occupation, goals) so the assistant's tone and suggestions are tailored to you from day one.
- Choose a personalization mode — Personal, Office, or Study — or create your own custom categories, and everything (conversations, tasks, chat tone) is organized accordingly.
- Light/dark theme.
- Manage your profile picture, friend code, and connected accounts.
- Turn "smart features" (proactive nudges, cognitive suggestions, reminders) on or off as a group, or individually.

## 12. Proactive Nudges
- The app can proactively check in — flagging an overdue task, a shift in your mood, or a friend you haven't spoken to in a while — and notify you before you'd have noticed yourself.

## 13. Adaptive Home Screen *(Mobile)*
- The mobile home screen subtly changes its animation feel (livelier, calmer, more minimal) based on your recent mood and the tone of your last conversation, plus the time of day — a small, ambient way the app reflects how you've been doing.

## 14. Works Offline, Feels Instant
- Your conversations, tasks, friends, and profiles are cached on-device, so lists load instantly even before the app has re-synced with the server.
- Messages sent while offline aren't lost — they queue and deliver automatically once you're reconnected.

---

*Both the mobile and web apps share the same account, conversations, tasks, friends, and history — anything you record or send on one shows up on the other. Native calling integration and the adaptive home screen are currently mobile-only.*
