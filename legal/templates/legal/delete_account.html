Delete your Throughline account

This can't be undone.

Deleting your account permanently removes it and the data stored with it:

  • your profile, profile picture and settings;
  • transcripts, conversations, notes, tasks, reminders, people profiles and insights;
  • your chats with the assistant, and files and images you uploaded;
  • your friends list, direct messages you sent and received, and your call history;
  • connected Gmail, Outlook and Swiggy access (we delete the stored tokens);
  • email drafts, expenses, insurance details, mood, heart-rate and TV activity, and Meet cards.

It happens straight away. Copies in backups, if any, are overwritten within 30 days. We'll send a confirmation to your account's email address. To keep a copy of anything, save it before you delete.

How to delete

In the app: open Settings and choose Delete account.

Here: sign in below and confirm.

Forgot your password? Reset it from the log-in screen first.

Can't sign in? Email {% if info.contact_email %}{{ info.contact_email }}{% else %}{{ val('', "Contact email") }}{% endif %} from your account's email address and we'll delete it for you.
